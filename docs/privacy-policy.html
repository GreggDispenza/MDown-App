MDown — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

MDown ("the app") converts documents you choose — such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, HTML and CSV files — into Markdown text. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

MDown does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All document conversion happens entirely on your device. Your files and their contents never leave your device through the app.

What the app accesses

  • Files you explicitly select. When you pick a document to convert, the app reads that file locally to produce Markdown. It does not scan, index, or access any other files.
  • No account, no sign-in. The app has no user accounts and asks for no personal information.

What the app does NOT do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No network transmission of your documents or their contents.
  • No collection of names, emails, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • No third-party data sharing.

Data retention

The app does not retain your documents. Conversion output is shown to you and is kept only if you choose to save it, on your own device, under your control.

Children's privacy

MDown is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
